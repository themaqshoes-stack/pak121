Turn any photo into a 3D STEP file,
right from your website.

Drop in this embeddable widget. It uploads to your CAD backend, runs background removal + contour extraction + extrusion, then returns a STEP file. Perfect for Gridfinity bins, custom CAD parts, and product configurators.
